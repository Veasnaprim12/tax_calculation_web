Patent Tax
A. What is Patent Tax?

Patent Tax is a type of annual tax imposed on each business activity of taxpayers under the self-assessment regime operating in Cambodia, based on the category of taxpayer and annual turnover.

B. Tax Exemptions

Patent Tax is exempted for the following:

State institutions, diplomatic missions, foreign consulates, international organizations, and technical cooperation agencies of foreign governments.
Activities of organizations established and operated exclusively for religious, charitable, scientific, literary, or educational purposes where no part of the property or income is used for private benefit.
Any business activity whose annual turnover is below the threshold for taxpayers under the self-assessment regime.
C. Patent Tax Rates
Taxpayer Category Annual Patent Tax
Small Taxpayer 400,000 KHR
Medium Taxpayer 1,200,000 KHR
Large Taxpayer 3,000,000 KHR
Large Taxpayer (Annual turnover above 10 billion KHR) 5,000,000 KHR

D. Obligations and Procedures for Patent Tax Declaration

1. Patent Tax Payment Obligations
   Patent tax must be paid annually from January 1 to March 31.
   Patent tax must be paid at the tax administration where the taxpayer conducts actual business activities, at partner commercial banks of the General Department of Taxation, or through the online tax payment system (e-Payment).
   For newly established businesses:
   During the first 6 months of the year (January–June): full annual patent tax must be paid.
   During the last 6 months of the year (July–December): only half of the annual patent tax must be paid.
2. Forms of Patent Tax Payment

Patent tax is determined as follows:

One business activity = one patent tax payment.
Multiple business activities = separate patent tax payments for each activity.
Example

A taxpayer conducting import-export, transportation, and hotel businesses must pay 3 separate patent taxes for the 3 different business activities.

Subsidiary activities related to the main business activity are considered as one business activity.
Example

A hotel with entertainment clubs, restaurants, massage services, fitness clubs, or other subsidiary activities under the same management and located within the same premises only pays one patent tax.

Taxpayers with branches, warehouses, factories, or workshops for the same business activity within the same province/city only pay one patent tax.
Taxpayers operating in different provinces/cities must pay patent tax according to each province/city where the business operates.
Note

Taxpayers with branches, warehouses, factories, or workshops located in different provinces/cities must pay the minimum patent tax applicable to each taxpayer category in each province/city where the business operates.

A. What is Special Tax?

Special Tax is imposed on certain goods or services that are considered luxury items, non-essential for daily life, or harmful to health, society, or the environment.

Special tax applies to the importation, production, distribution of taxable goods, or the supply of taxable services for use within the Kingdom of Cambodia.

Registration Tax

Registration Tax is a type of tax imposed on:

Transfer of ownership or possession rights of immovable property, or contribution of immovable property as company shares.
Transfer of ownership or possession rights of transportation vehicles of all types.
Transfer of part or all company shares.
Contracts for supplying goods or services funded by the state budget.
Legal documents such as company dissolution, company merger, and company closure documents.

This tax is created for the benefit of local government budgets to support infrastructure development. Registration tax is generally collected from the recipient of ownership or possession rights.

5.1 Registration Tax on Transfer of Immovable Property
Definition

Registration tax on transfer of ownership or possession rights of immovable property is a tax payable when there is:

Sale and purchase
Exchange
Donation
Contribution of immovable property as company shares

Filing and Payment Obligations

Taxpayers are required to file declarations and pay registration tax within 3 months after the date of the official transfer document.

Required documents include:

Ownership transfer documents issued by the provincial/municipal cadastral administration for registered property (with title deed).
Possession transfer documents issued by local land authorities for unregistered property (without title deed).

Tax must be paid at:

Provincial/Khan tax branches
Partner banks of the Ministry of Economy and Finance

For properties located in Phnom Penh:

Tax declarations must be filed at the General Department of Taxation (GDT).

For properties located in provinces:

Tax declarations may be filed at provincial tax branches or at the GDT upon request of the taxpayer.

Tax Rate, Tax Base, and Tax Calculation
Registration Tax Rate

The registration tax rate on immovable property transfer is:

Registration Tax=Tax Base×4%

Tax Base

The tax base is the total value of the immovable property including:

Land value
Building value

The property value is determined according to the following priority:

Official valuation tables issued by the Ministry of Economy and Finance.
Property value stated in the transfer agreement or other legal documents.

If tax incentives apply:

Tax Base=Total Property Value−Tax Incentive

Example of Registration Tax Calculation

Case:

Transfer of a flat house with:

Land size = 100 m²
Ground floor area = 64 m²
First floor area = 64 m²
Building age under 10 years

Property valuation:

Land value = 300 USD/m²
Ground floor value = 250 USD/m²
First floor value = 200 USD/m²
Step 1: Calculate Land Value

100×300=30,000 USD

Step 2: Calculate Ground Floor Value

64×250=16,000 USD

Step 3: Calculate First Floor Value

64×200=12,800 USD

Step 4: Total Property Value

30,000+16,000+12,800=58,800 USD

Step 5: Convert to Khmer Riel

Exchange rate:

1 USD = 4,000 KHR

58,800×4,000=235,200,000 KHR

Step 6: Calculate Registration Tax

235,200,000×4%=9,408,000 KHR

Final Registration Tax:

9,408,000 KHR

Tax Exemptions

Registration tax exemptions include:

Land concessions granted by the Royal Government for economic or social purposes.
Property owned by state institutions.
Property owned by diplomatic missions, foreign consulates, international organizations, or technical cooperation agencies.
Transfer of property within family relationships:
Inheritance
Between parents and biological children
Between husband and wife
Between grandparents and grandchildren
From parents to biological children and legally married children-in-law
First-Time Gift Transfer
Between parents and biological children
Between husband and wife
Between grandparents and grandchildren
From parents to children and legally married children-in-law
From grandparents to grandchildren and legally married grandchildren-in-law

Tax Incentives

Tax incentives for property transfer within family relationships include:

Between parents-in-law and children-in-law

200,000,000 KHR

Unused Land Tax
A. What is Unused Land Tax?

Unused Land Tax is an annual tax imposed on land with buildings or vacant land that is not being used and is located outside the geographical areas subject to Immovable Property Tax in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

B. Key Definitions
Owner

An owner refers to a person officially recognized by the cadastral authority or land authority from commune/sangkat level upward as having the right to use land either permanently or temporarily.

Unused Land

Unused land refers to land with or without buildings that is not being used.

C. Tax Exemptions

Unused Land Tax is exempted in the following cases:

1. Small Land Areas

Unused land with an area less than or equal to:

5 hectares

for one location is exempt from tax.

2. Land Over 5 Hectares

Unused land larger than 5 hectares may also be exempt if it satisfies one of the following conditions:

Agricultural land currently under cultivation certified by the land valuation committee.
Land serving economic activities of individuals or legal entities registered with the tax administration.
Land with or without buildings under lease agreements.
Land owned by the Royal Government or government institutions.
Community-owned land.
Economic concession land leased from the state for economic activities.
State-owned land leased to legal or natural persons under contracts for economic activities.
Land located in Special Economic Zones directly serving agriculture, industry, or services.
Land registered as enterprise property in education, training, or vocational sectors used for those purposes.

If such land is sold or no longer used for the intended purposes, tax obligations must be fulfilled according to Cambodian tax laws.

D. Tax Rate, Tax Base, and Tax Calculation
Tax Rate

Unused Land Tax is collected annually at the rate of:

Unused Land Tax Rate=2%

Tax Base

The tax base is determined according to land value set by the Land Valuation Committee.

A deduction of:

5 hectares (50,000 m²)

is allowed for each land location.
